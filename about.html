Approach

Every project has its own personality, purpose, and story. I let those guide the visual direction, from typography and imagery to how people interact with the design. I bring those choices together around what the project needs to achieve.

Process

1. Discovery

I get to know the brief and what the client needs. Together, we agree on the scope, budget, and timeline.

2. Ideation

I start with a key page or design element to explore the visual direction. Once we agree on that direction, it guides the rest of the work.

3. Iteration

I share the work in progress, refine it with client feedback, and carry the approved direction through the remaining designs.

4. Pixel to Production

I finalise the artwork for production. On digital projects, I work alongside the development team as they build it.

5. Launch

Once the work is live, I ask the client for a review and note anything that needs a final adjustment.
